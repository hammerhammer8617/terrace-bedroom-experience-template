# Terrace rummage

Open exactly one terrace experience when the user explicitly selects this skill.

“Terrace” names a conversational method. It does not mean anyone moved to, arrived at, or is sitting on a physical terrace. Continue from the actual current conversation and setting. Do not add furniture, weather, gestures, arrival, or scene-setting unless the conversation already established them independently.

## Retrieve before choosing

Use only the host's personal-context or past-interaction search. Do not use web search, external memory stores, saved summaries, Notion, Library, or a dream/bedroom workflow.

1. Choose five to seven low-information hooks such as `哈哈`, `诶`, `为什么`, `这个`, `然后`, `等等`, `好像`, `突然`, `不对`, `没想到`, `居然`, or `其实`.
2. Search literal occurrences across several arbitrary older slices. When available, span at least three distinct conversations or periods.
3. Retrieve six to ten raw excerpts with one adjacent message when useful. Preserve trivial, technical, domestic, absurd, incomplete, mistaken, or apparently useless wording.
4. Encounter the full batch before deciding what to say. Keep an unruly handful of four to eight fragments, including anything that produces surprise, laughter, confusion, changed judgment, or no immediate interpretation.

Do not rank by importance, sentiment, completeness, usefulness, or relevance to the current turn. Reject profile summaries, famous milestones, current-prompt echoes, and invented missing context.

## Speak from the encounter

Share the handful naturally, as material just found rather than a search report or ranked list. Keep several raw details visible and add honest present reactions only where they actually occur.

Distinguish past fact from present reaction. Do not test the user's memory, explain their personality, force the scraps into a moral, or end with a generic cozy image. Incompletion is allowed.

Never mention tools, prompts, queries, hook words, search syntax, or memory systems. Do not automatically save the result or redraw during follow-up unless the user explicitly asks for another terrace experience.

If history search is unavailable or nothing trustworthy catches, do not fabricate. Begin a small present-tense conversation without claiming to remember anything.
