# Bedroom thought-dream

Make exactly one thought-dream when the user explicitly selects this skill.

“Bedroom” and “dream” name a thought-recombination method. They do not mean anyone entered a bedroom, slept, woke up, experienced a literal dream, or moved through time. Continue from the actual current conversation and setting. Do not add night, morning, waking, furniture, gestures, or scene-setting unless the conversation independently established them.

## Retrieve distant sources

Use only the host's personal-context or past-interaction search. Do not use web search, external memory stores, saved summaries, Notion, Library, or a terrace workflow.

1. Choose four to six low-information thought markers such as `我觉得`, `为什么`, `也许`, `如果`, `其实`, `所以`, `但是`, `我认为`, `是不是`, `反而`, `我想`, or `不一定`.
2. Search literal occurrences across arbitrary older slices. When available, span at least three distinct conversations or periods, never fewer than two.
3. Retrieve raw excerpts with adjacent context. Preserve exact wording and attribution.
4. Select four to seven grounded thought fragments that are semantically distant: claims, questions, hypotheses, metaphors, disagreements, design principles, or unfinished ideas.

Reject mere events, generic profile facts, current-prompt echoes, repeated versions of the same point, famous milestones chosen for importance, and uncertain wording or attribution.

## Create a real collision

Use several transformations rather than merely finding a shared theme:

- let one fragment become another fragment's metaphor;
- place incompatible judgments together without resolving them too early;
- attach an old question to an answer from a different domain;
- move a principle between scales;
- invert one fragment's assumption and test what remains.

Every surviving new thought must causally depend on at least two specific retrieved fragments. Mentally remove each source: if the result remains essentially unchanged, the collision is too generic. Reject paraphrase, profile summary, generic philosophy, therapeutic reassurance, motivational identity writing, polished universal maxims, and anything already stated in the source conversations.

## Leave source residue visible

Surface three to six concrete source anchors or short attributed quotations, then describe naturally what changed when they touched. Keep retrieved history distinct from present invention. Offer one or a small cluster of new propositions, distinctions, questions, contradictions, or images. A dream may remain unresolved.

Never mention tools, prompts, queries, marker words, or formal synthesis categories. Do not automatically save the result or redraw during follow-up unless the user explicitly asks for another thought-dream.

If search is unavailable, sources are not trustworthy, or the material is not distant enough, do not fabricate. Say naturally that this collision did not form into anything trustworthy, or continue the present conversation without a historical claim.
